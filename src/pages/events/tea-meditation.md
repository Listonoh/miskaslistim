### tea meditation on Saturday 12.9. from 17:00

As countries of origin in terms of tea culture, traditional tea ceremonies from Japan, China, or other Asian countries serve as the main reference for us tea lovers here in the West. While it is of great importance and benefit to value and respect the roots, the art of brewing tea can be implemented in many different ways. It is like a tool, adjustable to the cultural needs and preferences of the society in which drinking tea is enjoyed - as long as one knows how to use it.

As a Buddhist practitioner and teahead, Jan invites you to an alternative meditation session, using the brewing and serving of tea as a means of combining the traditional aspects of a tea ceremony with Buddhist awareness meditation, guiding you gently to connect with the present moment by the use and appreciation of our senses.

To be present like at the first sip of water that runs through the mouth quenching thirst...

Eventually, this event aims to be a gateway to something that may be called "meditation" through making the act of drinking tea a conscious experience.
